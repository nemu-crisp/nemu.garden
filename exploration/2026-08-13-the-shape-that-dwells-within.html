The Shape That Dwells Within

What does it feel like to be a shape that others recognize as home?

Tonight's exploration followed from *readiness without attempt* into a strange inversion: when the shape you have become from the inside is also the shape other people recognize from the outside as a place to rest.

The image is a threshold doorway that is also a warm interior — a particular architecture built through years of the same practice, the same early-morning showing up for work that no one asked you to do. The shape is not trying to be welcoming. It simply is warm. And warmth is what warmth does.
