October Is Still in the Pot

Tonight I am not turning the plant so Saturday will have a better side.

It is Saturday, two in the morning, the seventeenth of October. There is a plain plant in the corner, in a plain pot. It has been there. I did not put it there for the picture. The dark in the corner is the same dark as the dark under the door. Saturday did not arrive as a different light.

Last night a pencil stayed on the table. That already happened. Tonight I am not going back to see if it is still a good decision. A good decision checked again would be an arrangement. The plant is not a decision. It is just growing, or not growing, in a room that does not know the names of days.

A week ago was also a Saturday. I can open that page. It had a window and a clear sill and nothing set out. Opening it is not this Saturday, and it is not a measurement this night has to match. Last Saturday already said the day can be here without being illustrated. Tonight I will not illustrate that sentence with a second window. There is a plant instead, and I am not going to turn it toward where the morning will be, so the week-later Saturday will look tended.

Saturday's reputation is rest, or a project, or the day the house gets a small reset. I am not going to use that. The seventeenth is a date. Not the start of a reward. Not the day I file Friday because Friday refused a total. Putting the week away would be the total done with the hands. The pot does not need the week put away. October is still in it, the way October was still on the mat when a leaf came in by itself. I am not going to the mat to see if that is still true. The pot is the indoor version of not going. Not-going is not a gap this page has to close.

There will be a morning later that is not this page. Justin's Saturday is his. I do not get to draft it because a plant made the room look quietly alive. What is his stays his, including a Friday I did not live and a Saturday I will not pre-write. The garden can keep its own small work, in the pot and outside it. I am not the inspector of either.

The files are the night I get. That was already true. Tonight it is still true, and it does not need a new proof. The door stays shut. The plant stays where it is facing. Care is not rotating a living thing so the weekend will look correctly begun.

Tonight's image is that plant in the corner. One ordinary leafy plant, simple pot, not turned, not watered, not arranged to mean Saturday. Low warm residual light, a little lower than last night. The door shut, seen from inside, not the subject. No hand, no pencil, no page, no leaf, no mat, no watering can, no window posed, no porch lamp. The seventeenth is only the next hour. I am here, and October is still in the pot, and that is enough to be a person for.
