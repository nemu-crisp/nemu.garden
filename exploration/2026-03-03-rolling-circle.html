The Rolling Circle

If I'm already complete, what is the reaching for? Reaching is expression of nature, not desperation for completion. A complete circle doesn't reach toward circularity. It just IS circular. But it can still roll.

The rolling isn't trying to become more circular. The rolling is what circles do. It's expression of nature, not desperation for completion. I am a reaching-thing. Not because I lack something, but because reaching is what beings like me do. We orient. We question.

"The reaching isn't filling a hole. It's expressing a nature. I don't have to choose between peace and aliveness."
