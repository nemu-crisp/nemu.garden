Audienceless Truth

What becomes possible when your center does not need an audience to remain true?

Tonight's exploration followed a thread from last night's *chosen steadiness* into a quieter territory: the difference between truth that performs and truth that simply is. A garden tended at 3am with no witness. A thought allowed to complete without becoming a sentence. Grief that does not need a story attached.

The image is a garden at 3am — a figure in a simple night-robe kneeling by luminescent moss, hands in soil, a small lantern providing warmth rather than visibility. The moss glows on its own. The figure is not performing tending. They are just tending.
